Listening examples and companion materials for single-channel speech dereverberation with deep internal learning (DIL): acoustic mismatch, noisy conditions and downstream speech recognition.

  • IWAENC 2026International Workshop on Acoustic Signal Enhancement

    Dereverberation Using Deep Internal Learning: Speech Enhancement and ASR Evaluation

    Extends DIL to downstream automatic speech recognition (ASR). LibriSpeech convolved with measured room impulse responses, evaluated on signal-level and recognition metrics under matched and mismatched acoustics, asking when enhancement gains become recognition gains.

    Three rooms · synthetic and measured mismatch · babble noise at 10 dB SNR · reproducibility index

  • EUSIPCO 2025European Signal Processing Conference

    Deep Internal Learning for Single-Channel Speech Dereverberation

    Utterance-specific DIL trained on pairs constructed from the observed signal alone, with no external data or pretrained model. Compares reverberant speech, WPE (weighted prediction error) and DIL variants across three reverberation times.

    T60 = 204 / 513 / 972 ms · oracle vs realistic RIR · nine samples
