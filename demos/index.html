Conference Paper · EUSIPCO 2025

Deep Internal Learning for Single-Channel Speech Dereverberation

European Signal Processing Conference (EUSIPCO), 2025

This work investigates utterance-specific deep internal learning for speech dereverberation using training pairs constructed from the observed signal itself, without external training data or pretrained models. The audio demonstrations compare reverberant speech, WPE, and DIL variants across different reverberation-time conditions.

A Study of Deep Internal Learning for Speech Dereverberation

IEEE Signal Processing Open

This extended study examines deep internal learning under matched and mismatched room impulse response conditions, including synthetic and measured RIRs. The companion materials include audio comparisons from different acoustic environments, details of evaluation datasets, and experimental configurations.

Conference Paper · IWAENC 2026

Dereverberation Using Deep Internal Learning: Speech Enhancement and ASR Evaluation

International Workshop on Acoustic Signal Enhancement (IWAENC), 2026

This paper extends our previous work on deep internal learning for single-channel speech dereverberation by examining its impact on downstream automatic speech recognition (ASR). Using LibriSpeech speech convolved with measured room impulse responses, it evaluates both signal-level and recognition metrics under matched and mismatched acoustic conditions, investigating when improvements in speech enhancement translate into better ASR performance.
