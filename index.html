Yuxi Zhang

Deep Internal Learning · Speech & Audio · Signal Enhancement

I am a PhD researcher in the Speech and Audio Processing Group at Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Patrick A. Naylor. My research investigates how neural networks can learn directly from individual observations, without external training data or pretrained models.

I am interested in self-supervised and test-time learning, speech enhancement, and extending internal learning to other signal restoration problems.

Research

My work explores internally constructed learning tasks for signal enhancement, from single-channel speech dereverberation to ongoing studies in bandwidth extension and ECG.

PhD Research

Deep Internal Learning for Speech Dereverberation

Developing utterance-specific learning from a single reverberant observation using an auxiliary acoustic degradation model. My work examines the relationship between training-specific and true room acoustics, with signal-level and downstream ASR evaluation.

Explore audio demonstrations ↗
Ongoing Research

Deep Internal Learning for Speech Bandwidth Extension

Investigating whether spectral relationships learned within observed speech bands can support reconstruction of unseen high-frequency content, including hybrid spectral band replication methods.

Ongoing Research

Deep Internal Learning for ECG Signal Enhancement

Exploring degradation-based and repetition-based internal learning, including beat-aligned masked reconstruction that exploits recurring cardiac structures.

MSc Research Project

Text-to-Image Generation with Stable Diffusion

Research on gender representation and sampling-based debiasing in text-to-image generation.
